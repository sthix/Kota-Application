    02 · The thinking

    Why this, and not something else

    Kota earns money when a company puts its benefits through Kota. The benchmark exists to start that conversation, so the change I'd make is the one that gets more visitors to ask for a quote, not the one that collects the most emails.

    Considered
    Cut the form down

    Fewer fields would lift completion, but a thinner benchmark is less useful to the user and gives Kota less to quote from. The questions are fine. Making people wait for the answer is the problem.

    Considered
    Visual polish on the report email

    A better email still arrives after the visitor has left. The best moment to show them their result is while they're on the page and paying attention.

    Chosen
    Answer first, price every gap

    One layout change, built on the data and form Kota already has. It fixes the drop-off at the email field and gives the visitor a clear next step: ask for a quote.

    03 · How I'd ship it

    Shipped as a test, not a bet on the funnel.

    The risk is that fewer people hand over their email. So I'd run it as a test and judge it on quote requests, the number that turns into revenue.

    Week 1

    Show the live panel to 50% of visitors

    Reuse the existing scoring logic on the client side, with no new data needed.

    Primary metric

    Quote requests per visitor

    Counted per visitor who starts the form. Secondary metrics: form completion and time to first result.

    Guardrail

    Contactable leads must not fall

    Email plus quote requests per visitor. If that number drops, put only the gap pricing behind the email.

    Week 3–4

    Read the result, then send gap data to sales

    "Your renewal is in March and dental is your biggest gap" makes a much better first call.
